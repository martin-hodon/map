Flags and coats of arms, written as text

The definitions are in data/flags.txt and the engine is flags.js. The map uses them in its info panel and its flag game. Click any of them below to open it in the editor, with what it uses and what uses it.

The language
a | b | cvertical bands, from the hoist to the fly, of equal width (France: blue | white | red)
a / b / chorizontal bands, from top to bottom (Germany: black / red / gold). / binds looser than |, so a | b / c is (a | b) / c
b*2a band's share, 1 by default (Spain: red / gold*2 / red)
( … )a part that is a whole flag of its own: white | (red / green)*2 (Madagascar). It can carry its own charges, centred in it and kept inside it: (crimson + gold castle size 0.5) | white (Castile-La Mancha), (blue + gold crown at 0.25,0.5) / red (Liechtenstein); there at and size are shares of that part
[h:w] [shield]at the very start: the ratio, height to width (2:3 by default; Switzerland [1:1]), or a shape: [shield] for a coat of arms, [pennant] (Nepal), [round]
+ … + …a motif: a definition that is only charges, with no field (it starts with +), reused by others as one charge: southern-cross: [1:1] + star size 0.14 at 0.5,0.17 + …, then navy + @southern-cross points 7 at 0.75,0.5. Placed like any charge, size 1 (the field's height) unless given, rot turning it about its centre and flip mirroring it (Hong Kong is one petal, @hk-petal, five times: + white @hk-petal rot 72 …); its charges that name no colour take the one it is given (+ gold @southern-cross), its stars that name no points those it is given
@CODEanother definition, as a field (each quarter of a coat of arms can be a flag) or as a charge (+ @ES-arms size 0.4, drawn in its own shape)
+ chargesomething drawn over the field, in order: + red disc. A charge starts with its colour (white if none is given; two for taegeuk and sun)
divisionsquarterly(a, b, c, d) · up(a, b) (split by a line rising to the fly's top corner: a above it) · down(a, b) (a line falling to the fly's bottom corner) · x(top, fly, bottom, hoist) (split by both diagonals) · stripes(13, red, white) · pales(9, gold, red) · checky(5, 5, red, white) (the second part charges alone makes a semé, them in every other square: checky(7, 6, blue, (+ gold fleur size 0.8)), France ancient) · lozengy(4, 9, white, blue) (diamonds, r rows and c columns; a fifth number turns them by that many degrees about the top of the hoist, the rows and columns then any numbers: Bavaria's bendwise lozengy(2.75, 2.2, white, cerulean, 39))
linescross · nordic (off to the hoist) · saltire · band (across) · pale (down) · bend (top left to bottom right) · rise (bottom left to top right) · pall (a Y on its side) · border · wave (across, n waves of height r: Kiribati). w is a line's width as a share of the height
shapesdisc ring star (points 7) stars 12 crescent (k, d) plus square diamond triangle (from the hoist; flip: from the fly) sun (n rays) wheel taegeuk script (writing, as a mere impression) triskele (three legs) trigram 101 (three bars from the top, 1 whole, 0 broken: Korea) poly x,y x,y … (corners, as shares of the width and height; more outlines after /, where they overlap a hole: poly … / …) · branch (a stalk from x,y to x,y, bowed by span degrees, to the right of the way from from to to — minus, the left — with n pairs of leaves and one at its tip, all alike: size a leaf's length, k its width (of the length), rot its angle off the stalk (40), w the stalk's width; Cyprus's olive branches, Eritrea's wreath)
emblemslion (rampant) eagle (Germany's) eagle2 (two heads: Albania's) eagle-at (Austria's) eagle-ru (Russia's) eagle-pl with eagle-pl-gold (Poland's: @polish-eagle) bear (Berlin's) horse (Lower Saxony's) harp with harp-strings (@irish-harp): traced from the arms' own drawings, simplified, their inner lines cut out; and plain outlines: castle tower fleur crown tree maple bird dragon trident shieldform. Busier emblems are motifs traced from the flags' own drawings and simplified, in their colours: @druk (Bhutan's dragon), @mx-arms, @sisserou (Dominica's parrot), @frigatebird, @tm-guls, @sa-shahada… A definition is kept under 5,000 characters (longer ones are reported): built of the parts above where it can be — a ring of stars is stars 12 r 0.33, not twelve stars placed one by one — and traced only roughly where it can't
fields as chargescanton( … ) (the top-left corner, size w,h), box( … ), shield( … ), circle( … ): a smaller flag drawn inside
modifiersat x,y (the centre, as shares of the width and height) · size s (a share of the height) or size w,h (shares of the width and height) · rot 30 (degrees) · flip flipv (mirror) · outline (the shape as lines w wide; a star as its line star: Morocco, Israel) · for stars: ring with r, row, from x,y to x,y, arc with r and span, grid rows,cols
coloursby name, each the usual shade on flags (near enough, not exact), or #rrggbb:
the fileCODE Name: definition, one per line (FR France: blue | white | red; the name is optional). Codes are ISO 3166 codes for countries (FR) and states (DE-BY), CODE-arms for a coat of arms, h365 for a state of the historical map (its id there) and h365.1923-11-12 for its flag from that day on (the latest not after the day shown counts; a year alone is its first day), or any word for a part used by others (castile). The definition - is no flag (h255.1935-09-15 Germany 1935-45: -: none drawn from that day). A line that starts with a space continues the previous one. # followed by a space starts a comment (#c8102e stays a colour)
