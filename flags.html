Flags and coats of arms, written as text

The definitions are in data/flags.txt and the engine is flags.js. The map uses them in its info panel and its flag game. Click any flag below to open it in the editor.

The language
a | b | cvertical bands, from the hoist to the fly, of equal width (France: blue | white | red)
a / b / chorizontal bands, from top to bottom (Germany: black / red / gold). / binds looser than |, so a | b / c is (a | b) / c
b*2a band's share, 1 by default (Spain: red / gold*2 / red)
( … )a part that is a whole flag of its own: white | (red / green)*2 (Madagascar). It can carry its own charges, centred in it and kept inside it: (crimson + gold castle size 0.5) | white (Castile-La Mancha), (blue + gold crown at 0.25,0.5) / red (Liechtenstein); there at and size are shares of that part
[h:w] [shield]at the very start: the ratio, height to width (2:3 by default; Switzerland [1:1]), or a shape: [shield] for a coat of arms, [pennant] (Nepal), [round]
+ … + …a motif: a definition that is only charges, with no field (it starts with +), reused by others as one charge: southern-cross: [1:1] + star size 0.14 at 0.5,0.17 + …, then navy + @southern-cross points 7 at 0.75,0.5. Placed like any charge, size 1 (the field's height) unless given; its charges that name no colour take the one it is given (+ gold @southern-cross), its stars that name no points those it is given
@CODEanother definition, as a field (each quarter of a coat of arms can be a flag) or as a charge (+ @ES-arms size 0.4, drawn in its own shape)
+ chargesomething drawn over the field, in order: + red disc. A charge starts with its colour (white if none is given; two for taegeuk and sun)
divisionsquarterly(a, b, c, d) · up(a, b) (split by a line rising to the fly's top corner: a above it) · down(a, b) (a line falling to the fly's bottom corner) · x(top, fly, bottom, hoist) (split by both diagonals) · stripes(13, red, white) · pales(9, gold, red) · checky(5, 5, red, white) · lozengy(4, 9, white, blue) (diamonds: Bavaria)
linescross · nordic (off to the hoist) · saltire · band (across) · pale (down) · bend (top left to bottom right) · rise (bottom left to top right) · pall (a Y on its side) · border · wave (across, n waves of height r: Kiribati). w is a line's width as a share of the height
shapesdisc ring star (points 7) stars 12 crescent (k, d) plus square diamond triangle (from the hoist; flip: from the fly) sun (n rays) wheel taegeuk script (writing, as a mere impression) triskele (three legs) trigram 101 (three bars from the top, 1 whole, 0 broken: Korea) poly x,y x,y … (corners, as shares of the width and height; more outlines after /, where they overlap a hole: poly … / …)
emblemseagle eagle2 (two heads) lion castle tower fleur crown tree maple bird dragon bear harp trident horse shieldform: simple outlines, with no detail (the lion, which many arms share, a little more: rampant, traced from Scotland's Royal Banner, its mane and inner lines cut out). Busier emblems are motifs traced from the flags' own drawings and simplified, in their colours: @druk (Bhutan's dragon), @mx-arms, @sisserou (Dominica's parrot), @frigatebird, @tm-guls…
fields as chargescanton( … ) (the top-left corner, size w,h), box( … ), shield( … ), circle( … ): a smaller flag drawn inside
modifiersat x,y (the centre, as shares of the width and height) · size s (a share of the height) or size w,h (shares of the width and height) · rot 30 (degrees) · flip flipv (mirror) · outline (the shape as lines w wide; a star as its line star: Morocco, Israel) · for stars: ring with r, row, from x,y to x,y, arc with r and span, grid rows,cols
coloursby name, each the usual shade on flags (near enough, not exact), or #rrggbb:
the fileCODE Name: definition, one per line (FR France: blue | white | red; the name is optional). Codes are ISO 3166 codes for countries (FR) and states (DE-BY), CODE-arms for a coat of arms, or any word for a part used by others (castile). A line that starts with a space continues the previous one. # followed by a space starts a comment (#c8102e stays a colour)
